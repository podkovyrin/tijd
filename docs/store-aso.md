# Tijd store positioning and keyword rationale

Research date: 14 September 2026. This is qualitative ASO research using public
Play listings, Google policy and the actual app. It does not establish search
volume, keyword difficulty, conversion uplift or ranking guarantees.

## English source, before translation

Title: **Tijd: Word Clock Widget** (22 characters).
Short description: **Time in words on your home screen. Choose your language and style.**
The full description is 109 words, down from 199. Uploadable source:
`android/fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/`. The installed brand stays Tijd.

“Yellow” is an editorial interpretation of the user's Spiral Dynamics request:
practical clarity, autonomy, adaptability and choosing what fits the context.
The language explains the system simply, gives people choices and avoids status,
superiority and universal lifestyle claims. It is not a psychological assessment
of users. “A clock that fits the way you read time” expresses that positioning.

## Search intent and field placement

| Term / concept | Intent and use |
| --- | --- |
| word clock widget | Most specific product category; title; “clock widget” in the first descriptive paragraph |
| time in words | Explains unfamiliar terminology; short description and core promise |
| home screen | Clarifies where the app works; short and full descriptions |
| text clock | Relevant synonym; feature bullet |
| transparent clock / customization | Feature intent; natural background, color and font wording |
| language / multilingual clock | Differentiator; language choice and accurate count of options |

Public examples: [Word Clock Widget – Text Clock](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.light.wordclockfree)
uses both category phrases; [Word Clock Widget](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=de.thinkofapps.wordclockwidget)
uses the same core category and describes time displayed as words. These are
vocabulary and positioning observations, not evidence of those keywords' traffic.
Tijd's verified distinctions are independent widget styles, broad written-time
coverage, two layouts and offline operation without ads or accounts.

Exclude “talking clock”, “speaking clock”, “alarm clock”, “world clock”, “live
wallpaper” and “language learning”: they imply functionality this app does not
provide. Avoid “91 languages”: the catalog includes scripts and regional variants.
No claim about price appears because pricing is not set. Do not append keyword
lists or competitor names to customer-facing copy.

Google Play has title, short description and full description fields, **no separate
subtitle or keyword field**. Limits are 30, 80 and 4,000 characters respectively.
See [listing setup](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152)
and [metadata policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842).
Keep keyword research notes here, outside uploaded metadata.

## Localization and measurement

Translate the clarified product intent, retain Tijd, and shorten titles and short
descriptions to local field limits without cutting words. Native terminology and
word order take priority over literal English keyword placement. The current translations were written directly from English and corrected
Russian context without translation services, then reread by their authoring
agents. Independent native-speaker review and market-specific search research
have not been performed. Do not claim localized keyword-volume validation.

After launch, use Play Console acquisition/search-term data to refine actual
queries. Test short-description messaging and screenshots with store listing
experiments where available; retain a clear control and evaluate conversion with
adequate traffic. No traffic data exists here yet. Suggested category:
Personalization. Screenshots should show real compact and single-row widgets,
then independent styling and language selection. Text-free screenshots can serve
multiple locales; prepare localized screenshots when they contain interface text.
